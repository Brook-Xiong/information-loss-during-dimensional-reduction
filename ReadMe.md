# Information Loss during Dimensional Reduction🌌

## 🌄Overview
This repository explores the phenomenon of **information loss** that occurs when data, systems, or even perceptions undergo **dimensional reduction** — such as in PCA, autoencoders, or manifold learning.  

The central question❓:
> How much information & meaning is lost and how much entropy does rises when higher-dimensional realities are projected into lower-dimensional spaces?

This project is not purely academic — it’s a **personal exploration** of how simplification, compression, and perception affect our understanding of truth, beauty, and existence.

---

## 🎯Goals
1. Quantitatively analyze information loss in common dimensional reduction techniques.
2. Explore entropy and reversibility: Can we *recover* or *approximate* lost information?
3. Connect computational entropy with thermodynamic and philosophical interpretations.
4. Reflect on parallels between **data simplification** and **human perception / consciousness** — how reduction of complexity shapes (and distorts) reality.
